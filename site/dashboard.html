A declaration graph, not a live one

Five places the old map implied more than the analyzer knows.

1 · Origin is a per-edge fact

Every edge carries static (declared in config or source, never seen running), runtime (seen running, never declared) or both. Line weight is that channel — hairline for declared-only, solid for agreed, and a loud rose dash for a shadow dependency.

2 · Confidence on every node and edge

confirmed means the parser cited a file for the fact; unconfirmed means it did not. Unconfirmed nodes get a dashed border and a washed fill; the detail card says why in plain words.

3 · Coverage sits under the map

A map with no coverage line reads as the whole system. The strip names how many files were read or skipped, and — when partial — says these counts are a lower bound in the same size as the counts themselves.

4 · The eleven kinds the analyzer emits

Each node kind carries a glyph so kind survives grayscale. external_api is the system boundary — the one node kind that is not yours.

▣servicelong-running HTTP/RPC process ⟳workeredge or queue worker ▤databaserelational or document store ≣queuemessage queue or topic ⌗kvkey-value / cache namespace ▥bucketobject storage ◷cronscheduled trigger ◈durable_objectsingle-instance stateful actor ↗external_apithird-party endpoint — the boundary ▭static_sitebuilt assets served as-is ⧉compose_servicecontainer from docker-compose
5 · A finding may have no recommendation

Recommendations are deliberately absent for rules where the finding says enough on its own. The panel shows it under "No recommended change" instead of an empty card or a generated suggestion.

How the three levels differ

Each level answers one question and hides everything that would compete with the answer.

L0 Which cluster is the problem?

Three boxes, three health chips each. All three lenses are visible at once here — the chips are how you decide which lens to switch to.

L1 Which node inside it?

Nodes and intra-cluster edges only. Edges carrying a high or critical finding under the active lens take the severity colour; line weight encodes where the edge came from.

L2 What does changing it touch?

One node pinned, its edges highlighted, everything else at 20% opacity. Dimming rather than hiding keeps the shape of the cluster visible.

Severity without colour

Three redundant channels, so grayscale print and colour-blind reading both survive.

1 · Hatch density

Critical fills at a 6px pitch, high at 7px, medium at 9px, low not at all. Density survives any colour transform.

2 · Glyph count

Two triangles for critical, one for high, a filled dot for medium, a mid-dot for low. Counting beats hue discrimination at chip size.

3 · The word

Nodes at high or above carry a text badge naming the severity. The 5px left stripe is the fourth channel — position, not hue.

Keyboard model

Every box is a real focusable control: Tab reaches it, Enter or Space zooms in, arrow keys move to the next sibling at the same level, and Esc goes up one level. Pinning is a toggle. Each box carries an aria-label and aria-pressed reports the pin state.

Why extraction cards show three legs

Any one of them alone is an argument for leaving things where they are.

The legs are the recommendation

"Extract a microservice" is the most expensive advice this tool can give. Only all three legs together mean a boundary already exists and the code has not caught up.

Two cards qualify, seven do not

Only nodes with all three evidence legs get the teal border. Configuration or single-file changes get a card without legs — the absence is a decision, not a gap.

Effort × impact, not a score

S/M/L against High/Medium/Low, shown as two separate chips with their own glyphs. Collapsing them would hide the only comparison that matters when picking this sprint's work.

Diff mode and export

One pulse, then stillness.

New findings pulse exactly once

A single stroke-width pulse on the node outline and one background fade on the panel card, both with animation-iteration-count: 1. After it settles, the "New" badge is the permanent signal.

Resolved items stay visible

Struck through, with what they were, in their own block below the live findings. Deleting them silently would lose the only proof that last sprint's work landed.

Reduced motion disables it

A prefers-reduced-motion block sets animation: none on both. The badge, teal outline and struck-through list carry the whole diff without the pulse.
