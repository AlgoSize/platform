Step 1 is destructive and intentional — the repo's own wrangler.jsonc header
documents this trigger as needing deletion.

STEP 1 — confirm then delete the trigger

  curl -s -X GET \
    "https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/$CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID/builds/triggers/7d4c3cb7-208e-44f2-ad70-ade2c7e30562" \
    -H "Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN"

  Confirm it shows: trigger_name "Deploy non-production branches",
  branch_excludes ["main"], deploy_command "npx wrangler versions upload",
  bound to external_script_id abe719a7c4074ee69b64dee06158d426 (= algosize).

  If it matches, delete it:

  curl -s -X DELETE \
    "https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/$CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID/builds/triggers/7d4c3cb7-208e-44f2-ad70-ade2c7e30562" \
    -H "Authorization: Bearer $CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN"

  curl -s -X GET \
